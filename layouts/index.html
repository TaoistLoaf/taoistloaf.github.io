陶渊明 · 《读山海经·其一》

孟夏草木长，绕屋树扶疏。

众鸟欣有托，吾亦爱吾庐。

既耕亦已种，时还读我书。

穷巷隔深辙，颇回故人车。

欢言酌春酒，摘我园中蔬。

微雨从东来，好风与之俱。

泛览周王传，流观山海图。

俯仰终宇宙，不乐复何如？

Early-summer grasses and trees grow tall; lush branches encircle my home.

The birds rejoice in having found shelter; I too have come to love my dwelling.

The fields are tilled and seeds are sown; now and then I return to my books.

A secluded lane keeps deep wheel-ruts away and turns old friends’ carriages aside.

Gladly I pour the spring wine and gather greens from my garden.

A fine rain comes out of the east; a gentle wind arrives with it.

I wander through the tales of King Zhou and gaze across the Mountains and Seas.

Looking up and down, I hold the universe—what greater joy could there be?
