I’m Zachary (Ziyue) Zhuang, a Computer Science master’s student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, graduating in May 2026.

My interests sit at the intersection of human-computer interaction, recommendation systems, and generative models. Recently, I have been exploring user experience and evaluation for diffusion-based image generation and 3D latent representation models.

I also enjoy building user-facing tools with LLMs and open APIs such as arXiv, Firebase, and Semantic Scholar. My work ranges from interface design to backend systems.

Away from the screen, I play tennis, cook, build LEGO sets, take flash-lit photographs, and play board games. This site is where I keep technical notes as well as more personal writing.

I’m open to full-time and research opportunities in human-centered AI and generative systems. Feel free to reach out.
